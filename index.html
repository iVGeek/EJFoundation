Our Story

Elsie’s Hope Foundation was created to honor the memory of Elsie Jelagat. Our mission is to raise awareness, provide support, and break the stigma surrounding mental health.

Our Programs

Mental Health Awareness Marathon

Annual community event promoting mental health education and support.

Children Home Visits

Providing support and mentorship for children in care facilities.

Elderly Home Visits

Companionship and mental health support for senior community members.

University Talks

Educational seminars on mental health awareness for students.

Civic Education

Community workshops on mental health rights and resources.
