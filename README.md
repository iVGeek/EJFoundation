# EJ Hope Foundation

## Overview
EJ Hope Foundation is dedicated to raising awareness about mental health and providing support to individuals struggling with mental health challenges. Inspired by the memory of Elsie Jelagat, the foundation aims to break the stigma surrounding mental health, promote well-being, and offer resources for those in need.

## Our Mission
Our mission is to create a world where mental health is openly discussed, understood, and supported. We strive to:
- **Raise Awareness**: Educate communities on mental health issues and the importance of seeking help.
- **Support Individuals**: Provide counseling services, peer support groups, and mentorship programs.
- **Advocate for Change**: Work with policymakers to improve mental health services and accessibility.
- **Empower Communities**: Equip individuals with the tools to manage their mental well-being through workshops and resources.

## Programs & Initiatives
- **Mental Health Workshops**: Free sessions on coping strategies, self-care, and resilience.
- **Counseling & Support Groups**: Safe spaces for individuals to share experiences and receive guidance.
- **Youth & Schools Program**: Collaborating with educational institutions to promote mental health awareness among students.
- **Community Outreach**: Engaging with local communities to provide mental health education and support.
- **Helpline & Online Support**: Confidential support for individuals in distress.

## Get Involved

- **Donate**: Contributions help us expand our programs and reach more people in need.
